What we do

We find the weak points, then help you fix and manage them.

Security audits

Security and performance audits of information systems, and audits of IT management and compliance against law, regulator rules and standards (ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 20000-1, ISO 22301). Includes personal data processing audits.

Cabinet Regulation 397 compliance

We help essential and important service providers meet Latvia's National Cybersecurity Law and Cabinet Regulation No. 397 on minimum cybersecurity requirements: gap assessment, cyber risk management documentation and the cybersecurity audit.

ISO 27001 implementation

Building an information security management system to ISO/IEC 27001 from gap audit to certification: risk workshops, internal audits, management reviews and support through the certification audit.

Cybersecurity manager as a service

An experienced cybersecurity manager (CISO) for your organisation as an outsourced service: risk register, supplier reviews, incident handling and staff training through the year.

Penetration testing

Black, grey and white box tests of applications, networks and whole organisations, with code review and social engineering where it is needed. OWASP and OSSTMM methods.

Attack surface assessment

Continuous discovery of what you expose to the internet, with exploitable findings verified by a security analyst before they reach you.

Risk and continuity

Risk identification, assessment and treatment planning with ISO 31000 and ISO 27005, plus business continuity planning.

Incident investigation

Investigation of security incidents and collection of evidence that can be used for internal decisions or handed to law enforcement.

Training

Certified courses in partnership with PECB and BDA, and personalised, individual courses built for each client: for implementation teams, internal auditors, and security awareness for all staff based on your own policies.
