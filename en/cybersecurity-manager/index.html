Service

Cybersecurity manager as a service

An experienced cybersecurity manager for your organisation without the cost of a full-time hire. We take on the day-to-day running of the security programme and report to your management.

Talk to us

Who it suits

  • Organisations that Latvia's National Cybersecurity Law requires to appoint a cybersecurity manager
  • Financial sector companies with DORA duties
  • Companies with an ISO 27001 system and nobody to maintain it
  • Companies for whom a full-time security lead would be too much

How it works

  • An agreed number of hours
  • One named manager with a team behind them
  • Work and evidence visible in Wingman
  • A quarterly report on what was done

What the manager does

  1. Risks

    Keeps the risk register and treatment plan current.

  2. Suppliers

    Assesses suppliers and the security terms in their contracts.

  3. Incidents

    Leads incident handling and reporting.

  4. Training

    Organises cybersecurity training for staff.

  5. Documents

    Keeps policies and procedures up to date.

  6. Checks

    Arranges penetration tests and audits.

  7. Reports

    Reports regularly to management on progress and remaining risks.

We have provided this service to a private pension fund since 2023, and earlier to an IT services company.

Frequently asked questions

Does an outsourced manager satisfy Cabinet Regulation 397?

The regulation sets qualification requirements for the manager and limits how many entities one person may serve at the same time. Before signing we check that these conditions are met in your case.

What if the manager is unavailable?

The manager has the Analytica team behind them, so knowledge of your organisation does not depend on one person.

How is this different from consulting?

A consultant gives recommendations. The manager carries out the security programme through the year.
