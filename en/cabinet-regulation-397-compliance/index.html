Service

Cabinet Regulation 397 compliance

Latvia's Cabinet Regulation No. 397, "Minimum cybersecurity requirements", has been in force since 2 July 2025. We help you work out which parts apply to you and meet them.

Talk to us

Who it applies to

  • Essential service providers
  • Important service providers
  • Owners and legal holders of ICT critical infrastructure
  • Suppliers whose clients ask them to prove they meet these requirements

What you receive

  • A prioritised assessment against the requirements of Regulation 397
  • An action plan with dates and owners
  • New or improved documentation
  • The audit report, where we are your auditor

How we help

  1. Status and scope

    We establish whether the National Cybersecurity Law applies to you, in which status, and which systems are in scope.

  2. Compliance assessment

    We compare the current state with the requirements of Regulation 397 and show what is missing.

  3. Documentation and measures

    We help write the cyber risk management documentation, policies and procedures, and put the missing measures in place.

  4. Cybersecurity manager

    If you have no specialist of your own, we provide a cybersecurity manager as a service.

  5. Independent audit

    We carry out the cybersecurity audit for organisations where we have not been the implementation consultant. Analytica is on the register of cybersecurity auditors.

Before Regulation 397 we spent many years auditing state information systems against the earlier requirements (Cabinet Regulation No. 442).

Frequently asked questions

What is Regulation 397?

It is Cabinet Regulation No. 397, "Minimum cybersecurity requirements", adopted on 25 June 2025 under Latvia's National Cybersecurity Law. It sets minimum requirements for essential and important service providers and owners of ICT critical infrastructure.

Does an ISO 27001 certificate mean we comply?

Not automatically. An ISO 27001 system covers much of what is required, but the regulation has specific requirements of its own. The assessment shows the differences.

Can Analytica be both consultant and auditor?

Not for the same client. To keep the audit independent, we do not audit what we helped to implement.
