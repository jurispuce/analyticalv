You can send questions about the processing of personal data through the contact form or by post to the registered address.

What data we process

  • Data you enter in the contact form: name, email address, organisation (if you give it) and the content of your message.
  • Data you give us when you contact us by phone or in correspondence.
  • Technical data that the hosting provider receives when you visit the site, such as IP address, browser type and time of visit.

Please do not include special categories of personal data or confidential information in the contact form.

Purposes and legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation).
  • To keep in contact with clients and partners and to keep the website secure: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as accounting requirements (Article 6(1)(c)).

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Recipients

Your data is seen only by Analytica staff who need it for their work, and by the processors that keep the website running:

  • Netlify, Inc. (USA): website hosting, and receiving and storing contact form messages.
  • Google Ireland Limited: delivery of the fonts used on the site (Google Fonts). When the fonts load, your browser sends your IP address to Google.

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is based on a European Commission adequacy decision or on standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep contact form messages and correspondence for as long as needed to reply and stay in contact, and no longer than two years after the last contact, unless the law or a contract requires a longer period.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or analytics tools.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to erase your data or restrict its processing;

  • receive your data in a portable format.

To use these rights, contact us. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (Elijas iela 17, Riga, LV-1050).

Personal data we process while delivering services to clients is governed by the contract with that client.
