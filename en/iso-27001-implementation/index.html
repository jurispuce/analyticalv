Service

ISO 27001 implementation

We build an information security management system to ISO/IEC 27001, from the first gap audit to the certificate. Since 2006 we have done this for state institutions, IT companies and a fintech.

Talk to us

When you need it

  • A client or partner asks for an ISO 27001 certificate.
  • You have regulatory duties (NIS 2, DORA, Latvian Cabinet Regulation 397) and want one system that covers them.
  • The company is growing and security can no longer be run from separate documents.
  • You are certified already but need to move to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 or prepare for a surveillance audit.

What you receive

  • Gap audit report and implementation plan
  • Risk register and risk treatment plan
  • Statement of Applicability, policies and procedures
  • Internal audit report and management review
  • If you wish, the whole system maintained in Wingman

How implementation runs

  1. Gap audit

    We assess the current state against clauses 4 to 10 of the standard and the Annex A controls.

  2. Plan and training

    We draw up an implementation plan with dates and owners, and train your team.

  3. Risks and controls

    We lead the risk assessment and help write policies and procedures and put the missing controls in place.

  4. Internal audit and management review

    We run the internal audit together with your auditors and prepare the management review.

  5. Certification

    We support you through the certification audit and help close any nonconformities.

We have helped state agencies, IT integrators, software houses and a fintech implement and certify ISO 27001.

Frequently asked questions

How long does ISO 27001 implementation take?

It depends on the size of the organisation and on what is already in place. After the gap audit we give you a realistic timeline and effort estimate.

Does Analytica issue the certificate?

No. An accredited certification body issues the certificate. We prepare the organisation and support it during the audit.

Can you also train our staff?

Yes. In partnership with PECB we deliver ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Implementer and Lead Auditor courses, and tailored training for implementation teams and internal auditors.
